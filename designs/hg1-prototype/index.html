SYNTHETIC DATA  ·  NON-PRODUCTION PROTOTYPE  ·  HG1 UNDECIDED  ·  NOT AN APPROVAL
This is an interactive prototype built to help the human product owner inspect the CP0 product/design contract in a browser instead of reading the underlying specification documents. It is based strictly on designs/FEATURE_DESIGN.md, specs/FEATURE_PRD.md, and decisions/product/PD-0004–PD-0008. Every number on every screen is hand-authored mock data — nothing is computed, fetched, or verified. Where the contract has a known, unresolved gap, it is shown here as a visible gap (see the "Contract Checks & Known Gaps" tab), not quietly resolved.
HG1 CP0 prototype — static, zero-dependency, no network calls. Source: designs/hg1-prototype/. Not an approval; HG1 remains undecided (reviews/gates/HG1_GATE_REVIEW.md).
