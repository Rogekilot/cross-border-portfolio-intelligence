Reading this block: boxed, clickable terms are citation chips — each opens the exact deterministic source behind that number or attribution. Plain, non-boxed connective words ("driven mainly by," "the item most worth your attention this week") are the AI's own framing language. The beta clause shows the rule this direction adds: a caveat attached to a chip's claim (here, Limited/short-sample) must appear as visible inline text next to the chip, not only inside its pop-out — a caveat must survive into the headline, not just the detail.
